## 🔬 Projects

**[Forecasting Intraday Volatility with Deep Learning](https://github.com/HashimKhan0/Research_Seminar)**
Supervised undergraduate research forecasting minute-level volatility of the US Oil Fund (USO). Adapted DeepVol, a dilated causal CNN, and benchmarked it against XGBoost, SVR, LSTM and TCN models. The research showed that the choice of volatility target mattered more than the choice of model.

**[Commodity-Currency Transmission](https://github.com/HashimKhan0/MMF1941)** · *in progress*
Tests whether commodity prices move commodity currencies on the same day but fail to forecast them, across AUD–iron ore, CAD–WTI, CLP–copper and NOK–Brent from 2010 to 2025.

**[Heston Stochastic Volatility Model](https://github.com/HashimKhan0/heston_stochastic_vol_model)**
Monte Carlo simulation and characteristic-function option pricing under Heston, exploring how mean reversion and correlation shape price paths.

**[Pairs Trading](https://github.com/HashimKhan0/pair_trading)**
Granger causality and Engle–Granger cointegration tests used to find mean-reverting stock pairs and build spread-based entry and exit signals.

**[SpaceX Landing Prediction](https://github.com/HashimKhan0/data_science_capstone)**
End-to-end data-science capstone covering API and web scraping, SQL, geospatial analysis, a Dash dashboard and tuned classifiers.
